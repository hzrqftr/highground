Privacy

Last updated: August 2026

What this page covers

Highground is a fan-made, non-commercial Dota 2 companion project. This page explains what happens to your data when you use the "Sign in with Steam" feature. Nothing here applies to browsing the site without signing in — that requires no data from you.

Signing in with Steam

Sign-in uses Steam's own OpenID login. You authenticate directly with Steam; Highground never sees, handles, or stores your Steam password or credentials.

After you sign in, Steam confirms your identity and Highground fetches your public Steam profile (SteamID, display name, and avatar) once, using the Steam Web API. That information is packaged into a signed session token and sent back to your own browser.

What is stored, and where

  • The session token (containing your SteamID, display name, and avatar URL) is stored in your browser's localStorage — on your device only.
  • Highground does not have a database and does not persist your profile data on any server.
  • The session token expires after 7 days. Signing out, or clearing your browser storage, removes it immediately.

Who this is shared with

Your data isn't shared with third parties. The only outside call made on your behalf is to Valve's Steam Web API, to fetch your public profile at sign-in.

Questions

This is a small hobby project, not a company. If you have questions about how sign-in works, check the source in the project repository.
